Direwolf 4 (direwolf4.png)
Original resource: Flydigi Space Station 4.2.3.1, .vite/build/assets/images/product/Controller/fp4/132/main.png.
Downloaded from the official software page: https://www.flydigi.com/index/down?nav_id=2
Installer: https://tencent-android.cdn.flydigi.com/PC/SpaceStation4.0/Flydigi%20Space%20Station_setup_4.2.3.1.exe
The original front-view transparent PNG is preserved as direwolf4-reference.png at its native 492x341 pixels. No claim of ownership of the source image or manufacturer's marks is made.
The high-resolution direwolf4.png, direwolf4-left-stick.png, direwolf4-right-stick.png, direwolf4-well-left.png and direwolf4-well-right.png are reconstructed assets at exactly 4x that reference size (1968x1364), not official photographs. They use the original alpha contours, sampled colors, vector logo and geometric gradients, without raster upscaling or neighboring-pixel patching. The reproducible generator is scripts/render-direwolf4.swift.

Edge cleaning (dualshock4, dualsense, xbox-series, extreme3dpro-base, extreme3dpro-handle)
scripts/clean-artwork-edges.swift removes the pale fringe and the cast shadow that cutting the subject out of its background left around the silhouette. It only recolours and re-weights the semi-transparent edge pixels and the outermost opaque ring; the controller itself is not repainted. ArtworkIntegrityTests fails if a visible fringe returns.
